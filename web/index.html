Company Policy Assistant

One question. Three ways to find an answer from the same 98 company policies.

What are we comparing?

Word search matches words and quotes policy text. AI with all policies sends the complete policy collection to Gemini. AI with meaning search (RAG) finds five policies using a saved vector index, then gives their text to the same Gemini model.

Saved evaluation: 14 questions, three methods

After refining the answer instructions, we tested each method once on the same 14 questions. The coding assistant checked responses against the policy text and expected behavior; student review is still required. These results do not establish general accuracy.
